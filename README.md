# NovaTech CRM Analytics with Amazon QuickSight

This project was completed as part of the Udacity AI/ML Scholars learning experience and the AWS Future Business Professional Nanodegree program. It uses Amazon QuickSight to analyze NovaTech customer, sales, marketing, and support data and to evaluate answers generated by Amazon QuickSight Q.

The project combines three related datasets through `account_id`, uses QuickSight dashboard visuals to investigate business questions, and documents how Amazon QuickSight Q answers were cross-checked against the dashboard. This demonstrates a practical business workflow: connect data, build decision-ready insights, ask natural-language questions, and verify AI-generated results against the underlying analysis.

## Learning Context

- **Program:** AWS Future Business Professional Nanodegree program
- **Learning community:** Udacity AI/ML Scholars
- **Primary analytics platform:** Amazon QuickSight
- **Generative BI capability:** Amazon QuickSight Q

The project applies skills from business analytics, data visualization, and responsible use of AI for decision-making. QuickSight Q is used to ask natural-language business questions, while the dashboard remains the visual source used to validate the answers.

## Project Goals

- Understand sales pipeline performance and deal value.
- Analyze marketing funnel activity, conversion, spending, and return on investment.
- Identify customer support trends and accounts that may need attention.
- Use Amazon QuickSight Q to ask natural-language questions about business data.
- Compare QuickSight Q answers with dashboard results.
- Practice validating AI outputs against source data and business reporting.

## Datasets

| Dataset | Description | Rows |
|---|---|---:|
| `novatech_crm_deals.csv` | Closed sales opportunities, account details, products, deal stages, and deal values | 499 |
| `novatech_marketing_campaigns.csv` | Campaign interactions, funnel stages, channels, costs, and revenue-related marketing data | 2,240 |
| `novatech_support_tickets.csv` | Customer support tickets, priorities, statuses, and resolution information | 3,000 |

The datasets use `account_id` as their primary connection. The data dictionary explains the fields, valid values, date ranges, and intentional orphan account IDs that do not appear in the CRM dataset.

## Amazon QuickSight Q Exploration

The QuickSight Q exploration log documents questions such as:

1. Which campaign channel has the highest conversion rate?
2. What is the average deal size by company size?
3. What is the average resolution time for critical versus low-priority tickets?
4. Which accounts have the highest support ticket volume, and what is their total deal revenue?
5. Are any campaigns spending more than they earn back?

## QuickSight Q Answer Validation

Amazon QuickSight Q's answers were compared with corresponding Amazon QuickSight dashboard visuals:

- Marketing Funnel: funnel stage by channel and spend versus revenue.
- Sales Pipeline: revenue by company size.
- Customer Health: resolution time by priority and at-risk accounts.

The documented checks found that QuickSight Q's summarized answers matched the dashboard results for all five questions. The log also provides a place to record disagreements, limitations, and when QuickSight Q should be used alongside, rather than instead of, a validated dashboard.

## Key Findings Documented in the Exploration Log

- Direct Mail had the highest reported conversion rate at 53.0%.
- Enterprise companies had the highest average deal size among the compared company-size tiers.
- Critical-priority tickets averaged 1.79 days to resolve, compared with 1.97 days for low-priority tickets.
- The ten highest-volume support accounts collectively represented 106,842 tickets and approximately $169.7 million in annual revenue.
- No campaign was reported as losing money; NovaPulse Launch had the highest reported ROI, while NovaEdge Awareness had the lowest.

## Data Notes

- The CRM dataset contains 85 unique accounts.
- Marketing and support records may include intentional orphan account IDs that are not present in CRM.
- CRM deal value is zero for lost deals.
- The data dictionary should be treated as the source of truth for field meanings and join behavior.
